Everything You Need

From filing a complaint to resolution and archiving — SICMS covers the entire case lifecycle.

Complaint Filing

Students submit complaints online with details, respondents, witnesses, and supporting evidence uploads.

Case Management

Track cases through statuses — Submitted, Verified, Resolved, Archived — with a complete history trail.

Hearing Scheduling

Schedule hearings with venue or Google Meet link, with a calendar view and status tracking.

Chat & Messaging

Secure, per-case messaging between students and SDRU personnel with file attachments.

Reports & PDF Export

Filterable reports by date, status, classification, and college — exported as branded PDFs.

SDRU Assistant

A rule-based chatbot answering common questions about complaint procedures and workflows.

How It Works

A simple and transparent process from start to finish.

1

File a Complaint

Submit your complaint online with relevant details and supporting evidence.

2

Review & Assignment

SDRU reviews the complaint and assigns a coordinator for investigation.

3

Hearing

A hearing is scheduled (on-site or online) and both parties are notified.

4

Resolution

The case is resolved with remarks and the outcome is recorded in the system.
